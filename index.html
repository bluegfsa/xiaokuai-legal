English

Scope

This policy covers the iOS app Smallblocks. Version 1 is offered on the App Store in the United States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, and via TestFlight in mainland China, under this same policy. Apple's own services (the App Store, iCloud) are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

We do not collect or request: your name, phone number, email, ID, or any account credentials — the app has no sign-in. We do not collect what you type (task names, steps, notes, time blocks) or any health information. We request no access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location, clipboard or biometrics. We use no advertising identifier and do not track you across apps or websites, so the app never shows Apple's tracking permission prompt. The app includes no third-party SDKs: no ads, no analytics SDK, no push provider, no social login, no payments.

What stays on your device

Tasks, steps, time blocks, completion history, whether a task came from a template (a yes/no flag — never the template's name or text), reminder settings and preferences are stored in the app's local sandbox on your device. Reminders are local iOS notifications, delivered by your own phone. There is no account server and no copy of your task content on our side. Deleting the app deletes all of it, permanently, with no request needed. If you have iCloud or computer backups turned on, iOS may include the app's data in your backup; that backup is controlled by your Apple account and encrypted, and we cannot access it.

Notification permission

Requested the first time you save a schedule that includes a reminder — not on cold launch — only to show the reminders you schedule. You can turn it off any time in iOS Settings → Notifications; reminders then stop, and everything else keeps working.

Anonymous usage counts

We receive event names (for example "task created"), a timestamp, app version, and a randomly generated install ID created on first launch, unrelated to your device identifiers or Apple account and replaced when you reinstall. We do not receive task text, template names, dates, or anything identifying you; events carry no text fields. We do not collect OS version or device model, and we do not log IP addresses or user-agent strings. Use is limited to counting feature usage: no advertising, no recommendations, no profiling of individuals. We do not sell it or share it with advertisers or data brokers. Events are queued on your device for up to 7 days; events received by the server are deleted after 180 days. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy → "Anonymous usage counts"; reporting stops immediately.

What we never do

No selling data, no ads or ad SDKs, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, no use of your content to train models (version 1 has no AI features), no access to contacts/photos/location/health, no silent upload of your task content.

Children

Not directed to children under 14. The App Store age rating is 4+ (a content rating, not a claim that the app is designed for children). A guardian can delete the app to erase all of its data, or contact us.

Not a medical device

This is a time-recording and reminder tool: break a task into steps, place them on a timeline, get a reminder when it is time. It does not screen, diagnose, assess or treat any condition and is not a substitute for medical or psychological advice.

Security

Your task content sits in the iOS app sandbox, protected by iOS sandboxing and file protection; task content never travels over a network. The only data that leaves the device is the anonymous usage counts described above, which carry no text. With no account, there are no credentials to steal.

Your choices

Access and delete: the data is on your device — deleting the app removes it. Turn off anonymous usage counts in Settings. Contact us for anything else.

Changes

Updates change the version and effective date at the top; older versions stay available. Any expansion of what we collect (for example accounts, cloud sync, AI in a future version) will be announced in-app and consented to separately, not just edited into this page.
